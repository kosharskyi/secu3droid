## The Bluetooth picker expands before its list scrolls

Keep the outer `ModalBottomSheet` at full window constraints and make its inner content fill the available height. With `skipPartiallyExpanded = false`, Material3 can then create stable half-height and expanded anchors independent of how many devices have arrived. Its native nested scroll consumes upward gestures to expand the sheet first; once fully expanded, further gestures scroll the weighted lazy list. Constraining the outer modifier before `draggableAnchors` changes the anchor reference height and can pin the sheet to the top. The outer modifier's test tag reports pre-offset bounds, so use visible content bounds when asserting the rendered anchor. Keep Material3's default safe-drawing insets; extra bottom padding or a spacer creates a second gap. Stable MAC keys preserve row identity, but do not prevent a content-sized sheet from changing its height and moving every row.
